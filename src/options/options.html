OpenNDM-Extension

Capture downloads and page media, then hand them to Neat Download Manager.

Connection

NDM listens on a local WebSocket port. Keep the default unless you changed it in NDM.

—

Start Neat Download Manager, then test again. The default port is 10007.

Capture

Only download-looking responses are taken over. Pages, scripts, images, and player segments stay in the browser.

Capture downloads Hand supported downloads to NDM automatically.
Capture video and audio Include direct media responses and HLS playlists.
Page media panel Show a floating panel with media found on the page.
Take over browser downloads Cancel Chrome’s copy after NDM accepted the task.
Minimum media size Smaller media responses are left to the browser.
MB

Hold Ctrl (⌘ on macOS) while clicking a link to keep that download in the browser.
